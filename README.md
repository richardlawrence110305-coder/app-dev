
# My Favorite Movie

## Title: The Lion King

**Genre:** Animation, Adventure, Drama

### About the Movie
The Lion King is a movie about a young lion named Simba who learns how to become a responsible king. He faces many challenges before returning to his home.

### Why I Like It
I like this movie because it has a meaningful story and teaches us about family, courage, and responsibility.

### My Favorite Character
**Simba** - He is brave and learns from his mistakes.

### Rating
5/5 stars
